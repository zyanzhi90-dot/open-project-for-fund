Centre for Artificial Intelligence and Robotics (CAIR) focuses on research on computational algorithms, vision and pattern recognition, natural language processing (NLP), human-computer communication, autonomous driving and intelligent robotics, which play a vital role in the development of Macao’s smart city. UM established the Joint Laboratory of Artificial Intelligence and Robotics with the Shenzhen Institutes of Advanced Technology of the Chinese Academy of Sciences, and further developed the UM branch of the national Super Intelligent Computing Centre in Hengqin. CAIR’s core research areas include the following five main research directions:
AI for Metaverse
AI for Portuguese Business
AI for Health
AI for Digital Entertainment
AI Robotics
AI for Metaverse
CAIR develops the new Metaverse core theory and principles for the interdisciplinary technology, and promotes the further scientific research achievements and industry products of Metaverse technology in the areas like smart tourism, intelligent transportation, autonomous driving, and smart education.
AI for Portuguese Business
To cooperate with Community of Portuguese Language Countries along “One belt, One Road”, CAIR assists professionals in handling Chinese-Portuguese-English translation tasks through conducting research on Portuguese business such as UM-CAT platform.
AI for Health
CAIR deploys AI system to better mine medicine data, as well as analyze medical image and integrate medical treatments with smart diagnosing and auxiliary learning system.
AI for Digital Entertainment
CAIR constructs the intelligent mechanism in human behavior analysis and depression analysis, multimedia-based entertainment and data security and GDPR.
AI Robotics
Artificial intelligence gives robots a computer vision to navigate, sense, calculate their reaction and learns to perform tasks from humans through machine learning.
