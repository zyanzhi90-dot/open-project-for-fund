The intellectual property rights owned by each Party prior to the implementation of The 
Project shall remain vested in the respective Party and shall not be changed by joint 
development or participation in The Project. For any c
ommercial use of existing

permission to use agreement must be signed.
 
2.
 
The ownership of scientific and technological achievements and intellectual property 
rights jointly dev

-
owned by completion Parties. 
The details shall be negotiated by the completion Parties and determined in writing 
according to each proposal through mutual negotiation. Any Party (including either 
Party in this Agreeme

operation must obtain written confirmation about the ranking and distribution ratio from
